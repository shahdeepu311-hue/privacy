Privacy Policy

Color Tap Frenzy · Effective date: 18 November 2026 (version 1.2)

In short: Color Tap Frenzy has no ads, no purchases and no analytics, and it does not use the internet. We (DeepVerse Studios) do not collect any information. Your player names, scores and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Color Tap Frenzy (“the app”) is a casual color-matching game published on Google Play by DeepVerse Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not run any servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

DataWhy
Player names you type (any nickname works) Keeps each player's scores separate when several people share one phone
Each player's best scoreShown on the start and game screens
Each player's game stats (games played, total points, best combo) Shown on the Stats screen
Sound on/off settingRemembers your mute choice

This data is never sent anywhere. We cannot access it. You can delete a player on the Stats screen, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app may use your phone's vibration motor for short feedback when you tap a wrong color; this involves no data. The app does not need internet access to play.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect in the app.
