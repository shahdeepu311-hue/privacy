Privacy Policy

Color Tap Frenzy · Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: Color Tap Frenzy does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no internet access. Your scores and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Color Tap Frenzy (“the app”) is a casual color-matching game published on Google Play by DeepVerse Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and it does not request internet access.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

DataWhy
Best scoreShown on the start and game screens
Game stats (games played, total points, best combo) Shown on the Stats screen
Sound on/off settingRemembers your mute choice

This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions. It may use your phone's vibration motor for short feedback when you tap a wrong color; this does not require a permission and involves no data.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we have nothing to share with or sell to third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for example, by adding advertising), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
