Privacy Policy

Color Tap Frenzy · Effective date: 17 November 2026 (version 1.1, with ads)

In short: We (DeepVerse Studios) do not collect or store any personal information on our own servers, and we have none. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to serve and measure ads, as explained below. Buying Remove Ads turns all ads off.

Who we are

Color Tap Frenzy (“the app”) is a casual color-matching game published on Google Play by DeepVerse Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos, and we do not run any servers that receive data from the app. We do not use analytics services.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

DataWhy
Best scoreShown on the start and game screens
Game stats (games played, total points, best combo) Shown on the Stats screen
Sound on/off settingRemembers your mute choice
Whether you bought Remove AdsKeeps ads off, even offline

We cannot access this data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show personalised ads where you have allowed this. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy settings on the app's Stats screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Buying Remove Ads stops all ads, so the app stops loading ads from AdMob.

In-app purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play only tells the app whether the purchase was completed, so the app can turn ads off. Google's handling of payments is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play. It may use your phone's vibration motor for short feedback when you tap a wrong color; this involves no data.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Data collected by Google AdMob for advertising is described in the Advertising section above.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect in the app.
